# Cellular Automata

  
**Version:** 0.5.9  
**License:** GPL  
**Release:** 10/18/2017  
**Authors:** Pedro R. Andrade  
**Depends:** terrame (>= 2.0)  
  
  

Implements the Cellular Automata paradigm.  

## [Models](models.md)

| Model | Description |
|---|---|
|[Anneal](models.md#Anneal)|Anneal model from Toffoli & Margolis (1988).|
|[BandedVegetation](models.md#BandedVegetation)|Banded vegetation model based on Dunkerley (1997) Banded vegetation: development under uniform rainfall from a simple cellular automaton model.|
|[DaisyWorld](models.md#DaisyWorld)|Implementation of a 2D Daisy World model.|
|[Excitable](models.md#Excitable)|Excitable model from Wiener & Rosenbleuth (1946).|
|[Fire](models.md#Fire)|A Model to simulate fire in the forest.|
|[Growth](models.md#Growth)|Simple growth model.|
|[InterspecificCompetition](models.md#InterspecificCompetition)|Spatial Interspecific Competition using CA.|
|[Life](models.md#Life)|A Model to simulate Game of Life.|
|[Oscillator](models.md#Oscillator)|Oscillator model from Ermentrout & Edelstein-Keshet (1993).|
|[Parasit](models.md#Parasit)|Parasit model from Hassell et al.|
|[Parity](models.md#Parity)|Parity model, by Nigel Gilbert.|
|[Snow](models.md#Snow)|Snow falling from the sky.|
|[SolidDiffusion](models.md#SolidDiffusion)|This model describes how diffusion occurs between two adjacent solids.|
|[Wolfram](models.md#Wolfram)|Implements Wolfram's one-dimensional Cellular Automata.|

| File | Description |
|---|---|
|[**25P3H1.life**](./functions/data.md#25P3H1.life)|25P3H1V0.|

|[**boatTie.life**](./functions/data.md#boatTie.life)|Boat-tie (or bi-boat) is a small still life whose name is a pun on 'bow tie'.|
|[**bookend.life**](./functions/data.md#bookend.life)|Bookend (or hook) is generation 1 of century and an induction coil.|
|[**dinnerTable.life**](./functions/data.md#dinnerTable.life)|Dinner table is a period 12 oscillator.|
|[**figureEight.life**](./functions/data.md#figureEight.life)|Figure eight (or less frequently, big beacon) is a period 8 oscillator found by Simon Norton in 1970.|
|[**glider.life**](./functions/data.md#glider.life)|A glider (or featherweight spaceship) is the smallest, most common, and first-discovered spaceship.|
|[**heavySpaceship.life**](./functions/data.md#heavySpaceship.life)|The heavyweight spaceship (or HWSS for short) is the fourth most common spaceship (after the glider, lightweight spaceship and middleweight spaceship).|
|[**octagon.life**](./functions/data.md#octagon.life)|Octagon 2 was the first known period 5 oscillator, discovered in 1971 independently by Sol Goodman and Arthur Taber.|
|[**pentaDecathlon.life**](./functions/data.md#pentaDecathlon.life)|Pentadecathlon (or PD) is a period 15 oscillator that was found in 1970 by John Conway while tracking the history of short rows of cells (see one cell thick pattern).|
|[**pulsar.life**](./functions/data.md#pulsar.life)|Pulsar is, despite its size, the fourth most common oscillator (and by far the most common of period greater than 2).|
|[**rPentomino.life**](./functions/data.md#rPentomino.life)|The R-pentomino is a methuselah that was found by John Conway in 1970.|
|[**rabbits.life**](./functions/data.md#rabbits.life)|Rabbits is a methuselah that was found by Andrew Trevorrow in 1986.|
